Your solo adventure starts here

Explore hex maps, fill in character sheets with Apple Pencil, ask the oracle, roll the dice, and write the story as it unfolds. All in one app for iPad and iPhone.

Free to start · iPad and iPhone · Coming soon to EU storefronts

No group? No Game Master? No problem.

In solo play you're both the player and the GM. Whenever you'd ask "What's behind this door?", ask the oracle or roll on a table instead, and see where the story goes. Eremite keeps the map, the dice, and the story together, so you can stay in the adventure.

Maps

Hex maps you can draw on

Paint terrain, fill whole regions, and keep unexplored hexes hidden until you reach them. Add notes to any hex, move tokens, and sketch right on the map with Apple Pencil.

Play

Ask the oracle, roll the dice

A yes/no oracle, a dice roller for any expression, your own random tables, PbtA moves, progress tracks, doom dice, and a turn tracker. Switch on just the tools your game needs.

Random tables

Build your own collection of random tables

Every table you make goes into one library, sorted by category. Choose which tables each campaign uses, roll on them from Play, and every result lands in your journal.

Start from scratch

Pick any die from a d2 to a d100, including 2d6 and d66, and fill in the results.

Paste it from a book

Type or paste a table from a rulebook, PDF, or module as plain text, and Eremite reads the name, die, and every row. Bring in a whole batch from a text file at once.

Combine tables

Roll two or more tables together as one result, like a treasure's type and material.

Make one up mid-game

Jot down a quick table in Play without leaving the session, and save it to your library if it's a keeper.

Journal

The story writes itself down

Every roll and oracle answer is logged for you, next to your typed, handwritten, or voice-memo entries.

Characters

Write on your character sheet

Import the PDF or a photo of any character sheet and fill it in with Apple Pencil, just like on paper. Track hit points, scribble notes, erase, and write again. Or start from a built-in template for your system.

Your campaigns go where you go

Start on iPad, continue on iPhone

Export a whole campaign to a single file and open it on your other device with AirDrop, Files, or Messages. Or keep the file as a backup.

Your journal, in Markdown

Export your journal as Markdown with its images and voice memos, ready for Obsidian or any notes app.

Sketching

A freeform board for dungeon sketches and mood boards, with images, text notes, tokens, and ink.

Any system

Built for OSR and indie games, and works with any ruleset you bring to the table.
