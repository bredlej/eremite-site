Play tabletop RPGs solo, anywhere

Roll the dice, ask the oracle, explore the map, and write down what happens.

Free to start

How solo play works

You play your character and run the game yourself. When you'd normally ask the GM "What's behind this door?", you ask the oracle or roll on a table, and the answer tells you what happens next. Eremite keeps your map, dice, and notes in one place while you play.

Maps

Hex maps you can draw on

Paint terrain, fill whole regions, and draw roads and rivers. Move tokens and sketch right on the map with Apple Pencil.

Hex details

Name and mark every hex

Mark individual hexes with names, short labels, and symbols, and link any hex to an article in your campaign wiki.

Sci-fi and modern

Play beyond fantasy

Eremite works for sci-fi and modern settings too. Map a star sector or a city with space and city terrain, and use tokens for ships, crews, vehicles, and people.

Play

Ask the oracle, roll the dice

A yes/no oracle, a dice roller for any expression, your own random tables, PbtA moves, progress tracks, doom dice, and a turn tracker. Switch on just the tools your game needs, and open them from any screen.

Random tables

Build your own collection of random tables

Build up one collection of random tables and choose which of them each campaign uses.

Start from scratch

Pick any die from a d2 to a d100, including 2d6 and d66, and fill in the results.

Paste it from a book

Type or paste a table from a rulebook, PDF, or module as plain text, and Eremite reads the name, die, and every row. Bring in a whole batch from a text file at once.

Combine tables

Roll two or more tables together as one result, like a treasure's type and material.

Make one up mid-game

Jot down a quick table in Play without leaving the session, and save it to your library if it's a keeper.

Journal

Every roll goes in the journal

Every roll and oracle answer is logged for you, next to your typed, handwritten, or voice-memo entries.

Sketching

Sketch your dungeon

A freeform board for dungeon maps and mood boards. Draw rooms with Apple Pencil on a grid, add images, notes, and tokens, and save a snapshot of the board to your journal.

Lexicon

A wiki for your campaign

Write articles for the places, people, factions, and items in your game, and link them with [[double brackets]] from articles and journal entries. Every article shows where it is on the map and where it's mentioned.

Characters

Write on your character sheet

Import the PDF or a photo of any character sheet and fill it in with Apple Pencil, just like on paper. Track hit points, scribble notes, erase, and write again. Or start from a built-in template for your system.

Take your campaign to another device

Start on iPad, continue on iPhone

Export a whole campaign to a single file and open it on your other device with AirDrop, Files, or Messages. Or keep the file as a backup.

Your journal, in Markdown

Export your journal as Markdown with its images and voice memos, ready for Obsidian or any notes app.

Party and NPCs

Keep a sheet for every hero in your party and every NPC they meet.

Any system

Built for OSR and indie games, and works with any ruleset.

Made by hand

Drawn by hand, not generated

The art in Eremite, from the dice to the fantasy terrain and tokens, is drawn by hand. The sci-fi and modern icons come from Apple's SF Symbols for now. And there are no AI features in the app: no generated tables, art, or stories, and no chatbot.
