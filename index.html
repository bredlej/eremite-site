Eremite

A solo RPG companion for iPad and iPhone. Ask the oracle, roll the dice, explore hex maps, and journal your adventure.

Coming soon to the App Store

No group? No Game Master? No problem. In solo play you're both the player and the GM. Whenever you'd ask "What's behind this door?", ask the oracle or roll on a table instead, and see where the story goes. Eremite keeps all of it in one place.

Play

A yes/no oracle, a dice roller for any expression, your own random tables, progress tracks, doom dice, and a turn tracker.

Journal

Every roll and oracle answer is logged automatically, next to your typed, handwritten, or voice-memo entries.

Hex maps

Paint terrain, fill whole regions, add notes to any hex, move tokens, and draw with Apple Pencil.

Sketching

A freeform board for dungeon sketches and mood boards, with images, text notes, tokens, and ink.

Characters

Sheets from built-in system templates or your own, or import a PDF or photo and write on it.

Any system

Built for OSR and indie games, and works with any ruleset you bring to the table.

Free to play with one campaign, one map, and one sketch. A single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited campaigns, maps, and sketches. No subscription, no ads, no account, no tracking.
