Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Tap Take the App Tour on the Campaigns tab (or in Settings) for a short walkthrough of every tool.

What does the one-time unlock include?

The free version includes every tool, with one campaign, one map, and one sketch. The unlock removes those limits, so you can have unlimited campaigns, maps, and sketches. It's a single purchase, not a subscription.

I bought the unlock. How do I restore it on a new device?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is my data stored? How do I back it up?

Everything you create in Eremite is stored on your device. It's included in your regular iCloud or computer backups of the device. You can also back up a single campaign: on the Campaigns tab, long-press it (or swipe right) and choose Export Campaign…, then save the file to Files. To keep a readable copy of your journal, export it from the Journal tab as Markdown, together with its images and voice memos.

Can I move a campaign between my iPad and iPhone?

Yes. On the Campaigns tab, long-press the campaign (or swipe right) and choose Export Campaign…. Send the file with AirDrop, save it to Files, or attach it to a message, then open it on the other device. If that device already has an older copy of the campaign, importing replaces it. There's no automatic syncing, so export again whenever you switch devices.
